Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

DealDrop ("we", "us", "our") is a deal-curation platform that aggregates product deals from major retailers including Amazon, Walmart, Target, and Best Buy. We do not sell products directly — we earn a commission when you purchase through our affiliate links.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following when you use DealDrop:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or create an account
  • Usage data — pages visited, links clicked, browser type, and device info (via standard server logs)
  • Review content — name, email, and review text when you submit a product review

We do not collect payment information. All purchases happen on third-party retailer websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you deal newsletters (only if you subscribed)
  • To display your reviews on product pages
  • To improve site performance and user experience
  • To send password reset emails when requested

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Affiliate Disclosure

DealDrop participates in affiliate programs including Amazon Associates. When you click a "Shop Now" link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps keep DealDrop free.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email and account data for as long as you have an active account. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@dealdrop.com.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@dealdrop.com or via our support page.
